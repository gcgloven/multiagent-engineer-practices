# Inquiry ledger

## Current frame

Question/topic; mode; intended understanding or decision; search/corpus boundary; resource envelope when relevant.

## Checkpoint

Learning; reframed questions; branches continued or set aside and why; whether deeper study, synthesis, or a decision is useful next.

## Synthesis and next decision

Findings and evidence; alternative explanations; unresolved questions and limits; recommendation or deferral; next decision. The recommendation may favor further inquiry or no action. Reconcile requested outputs with delivered evidence, distinguishing proposed experiments from actual runs. Use this material to write a substantive inline discussion, not merely to announce that the ledger is complete.
