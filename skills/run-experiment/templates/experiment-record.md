# Experiment record

## Purpose and mode

Exploration / confirmation / reproduction / behavioral prototype; question and intended decision; hypothesis when applicable.

## Design

Experimental unit; variables and controls; baseline; workload; invariants; sampling/repetitions and rationale; resource boundary.

| Metric | Definition and unit | Population / window | Failure or exclusion treatment | Decision role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Reproduction inputs

Code revision, data/version, configuration, environment, seeds, commands, outputs, and tool versions affecting interpretation.

## Execution and analysis

Instrument checks; completed/failed/excluded runs and reasons; deviations; exploratory follow-ups; executable transformations; uncertainty and sensitivity.

## Conclusion and review

Claim and tested scope; supporting/contrary evidence; assumptions; implementation/method/inference verdicts as needed; reviewer and evidence.

## Next direction

Continue discovery, reframe, confirm, reproduce, synthesize, stop, or adopt. For engineering transfer: invariants, limits, acceptance checks, and remaining operational requirements.
