# Requirements / PRD brief

Use this for substantial engineering work that needs a shared requirements basis. Extend an existing authoritative project record when possible; retain valid sections and link their exact revisions. This is a decision artifact, not a requirement to create a separate file for every phase. Use the caller's terminology and identifiers.

- Current status: exploring, proposed, accepted for a named scope, or needs a specified decision:

- Stakeholders and their present task, problem or unmet need:
- Evidence of that problem; distinguish observations from assumptions:
- Concrete intended journey: trigger → input → behavior → useful result:
- Important failure, empty or recovery path:
- Scope and explicit non-goals:

## Behavior and domain rules

| Requirement or rule | Observable behavior / data meaning | Source and decision status | Relevant consumer or owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include relevant states, units, ordering, identity, permissions, invalid-input behavior, compatibility and migration constraints. Mark unknown contracts explicitly; do not invent stakeholder agreement.

## Acceptance and quality envelope

| Outcome or quality constraint | Observable acceptance | Workload / environment / measurement method | Evidence needed | Status and decision owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use numbers only when justified or clearly proposed. An unconfirmed target can guide an experiment, but its result cannot be reported as accepted performance. State which constraints apply to the prototype and which must be established for the intended operating target.

## Choices, assumptions and open questions

| Question or assumption | Why it matters | Blocks which action? | Accepted by / evidence, or decision owner | Next probe or revisit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Separate a confirmed requirement, an explicitly accepted temporary assumption, and a proposed assumption awaiting a decision. An exploratory spike has a learning objective and boundary; it does not resolve unrelated product choices by default.
